# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in InFlow English to be safe and respectful regardless of experience, identity, disability, language background or communication style.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss observable behavior and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Make bug reports reproducible without pressuring anyone to expose private videos, credentials or learning records.
- Respect accessibility needs and different ways of processing written information.
- Accept correction when new evidence changes a technical or product judgment.
- Keep learning claims proportional to the evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, publishing private data, credential sharing, or deliberately misleading security and learning claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and users.

This compact policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and may be replaced by its full current text as the contributor community grows.
